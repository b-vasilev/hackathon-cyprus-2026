# ownership
Goal: show who can answer questions, maintain boundaries and validate this draft.
Use: CODEOWNERS, maintained contacts, team docs, support docs and explicit ownership declarations.
Prepare: documented ownership for product/components/data/operations; supported escalation or contribution routes; open questions grouped so a reviewer knows what to confirm. Preserve unknowns instead of assigning responsibility by guess.
Select: owner and component objects plus ownership relations and claims.
Visual: an ownership graph only when explicit owner-to-boundary relations exist.
Missing evidence: a commit author is not proof of ownership. Do not invent people, teams, contact details or approval status. Generated content remains a draft.
