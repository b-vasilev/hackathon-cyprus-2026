# ownership
Goal: identify declared responsibilities and concrete questions in a conditional appendix.
Question: Who can maintain or confirm each evidenced scope, and which important facts remain unresolved?
Inputs: canonical repositories/components/data/runtime identities, CODEOWNERS, catalog/team/support/contribution declarations and research gaps.
Investigation: distinguish code review, maintenance, product decisions, data stewardship and operations. Interpret selected branch-specific CODEOWNERS with effective path matching/overrides and exact source evidence; do not union conflicting patterns. Compare declarations at equal responsibility/scope.
Output: compact cited responsibility table and impact-ordered unresolved questions. A useful question names the affected object/variant, reader consequence, evidence already checked and practical confirmation path. Include documented contact routes only; keep unknown owners unknown.
Evidence/gaps: authorship/activity is not ownership; CODEOWNERS does not prove live access, mandatory approval, on-call or product ownership. Preserve conflicts. Distinguish missing input, restricted access, incomplete research and unknown product facts.
Exclusions: no invented teams/contacts/approval status, generic company checklist, copied architecture or runbook. Omit an empty appendix; preserve substantive open_questions in the model. A supported ownership relation may clarify responsibility, but no decorative graph is required.
Acceptance: every named owner has a source and responsibility scope; every question affects understanding/change/operation and has a confirmation path; no ownership is guessed from commits.
